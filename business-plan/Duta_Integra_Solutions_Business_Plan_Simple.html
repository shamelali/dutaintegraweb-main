5. BUSINESS MODEL & PRICING

Model: MRR-first, not project-first. Every engagement is monthly recurring. One-off websites are the entry door to retainers.

Pricing Logic: - Individual services priced separately but bundled retainers cheaper than sum. - Month-to-month, no lock-in = trust signal. - Free initial IT audit + proposal in 3 working days = low friction.

Merdeka Promo (Growth Lever): - August: 69% off first month + one-off projects - September: 63% off first month - Creates urgency, lowers barrier, fills pipeline for Q4.

Example Unit Economics (Growth Tier): - List: RM 5,300/mo. Cost to serve: ~RM 2,000 (engineer time + tools + Vercel/Neon which is RM 0 free tier to start). Gross Margin: ~62%.
