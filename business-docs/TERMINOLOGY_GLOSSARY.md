## Usage Guidelines

1. **Always use the approved terminology** — Do not create new translations for established terms
2. **Legal documents** — Must use exact legal terminology; have translations reviewed by legal counsel
2. **Consistency** — Use the same BM term throughout a document and across related documents
3. **Capitalization** — Follow Malaysian conventions (e.g., "Syarikat" capitalized in formal docs)
4. **Numbers** — Use RM for currency (e.g., RM 10,000.00), metric units
5. **Dates** — Format: DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 31/12/2025) or DD Month YYYY (e.g., 31 Disember 2025)
