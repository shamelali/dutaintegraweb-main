2. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a business relationship involving IT managed services, AI solutions, digital transformation, and related consulting ("Purpose"). This Agreement governs the exchange of Confidential Information for that Purpose.

3. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by inspection, including but not limited to:

  • Technical data: architectures, algorithms, source code, APIs, system designs, security configurations.
  • Business data: financials, pricing, customer lists, pipeline, strategies, roadmaps, metrics.
  • Personal data: any data subject to Malaysia PDPA 2010 (as amended).
  • Trade secrets, know-how, methodologies, frameworks, proprietary tools.
  • The terms of this Agreement and any related proposals, contracts, or negotiations.

Confidential Information includes information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

  • Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party.
  • Was known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure (evidenced by written records).
  • Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information.
  • Is rightfully received from a third party without breach of obligation.

5. Obligations of Receiving Party

5.1 Use Restriction

Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose. Do not use for any other purpose, including competitive analysis or product development outside the engagement.

5.2 Protection Standard

Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used for your own similar information (no less than reasonable care).

5.3 Need-to-Know Access

Disclose only to employees, contractors, or advisors who: (a) need to know for the Purpose, (b) are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

5.4 No Reverse Engineering

Do not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive source code from any software or systems provided.

6. Permitted Disclosures

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, regulation, or court order, provided that:

  • The Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice (where legally permissible).
  • The Receiving Party cooperates to seek protective treatment or confidential handling.
  • Only the legally compelled portion is disclosed.

7. Return & Destruction

Upon termination of discussions, completion of the Purpose, or written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (including copies) and certify compliance in writing. One archive copy may be retained by legal counsel solely for compliance purposes.

8. Term & Survival

8.1 Term

This Agreement remains in effect for 3 years from the date of signing, or until the completion of the Purpose, whichever is later.

8.2 Survival

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for 3 years (or indefinitely for trade secrets under applicable law).

9. No License

Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, right, or interest in Confidential Information or intellectual property, except the limited right to use for the Purpose.

10. Remedies

The Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief (without proving damages or posting bond) for any actual or threatened breach, in addition to all other remedies at law or equity. The Receiving Party acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm.

11. General

11.1 Governing Law

Laws of Malaysia. Courts of Selangor have exclusive jurisdiction.

11.2 Entire Agreement

This Agreement supersedes all prior discussions on confidentiality. Amendments require written consent.

11.3 Assignment

No assignment without prior written consent (not unreasonably withheld).

11.5 Notices

Notices via email to the addresses in Section 1 are deemed received upon sending.

11.5 Severability

If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect.
