1. Services

1.1 Services. Provider shall perform the services described in each Statement of Work ("SoW") executed by the Parties. Each SoW shall reference this Agreement and, upon execution, shall be incorporated herein by reference.

1.2 Scope Changes. Any change to the scope of Services shall be documented in a written Change Order signed by both Parties.

1.3 Standards. Provider shall perform Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with industry standards.

2. Term and Termination

2.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for {{TERM_MONTHS}} months unless terminated earlier per this Section.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days written notice.

2.3 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 30 days; (b) becomes insolvent or bankrupt; (c) ceases business operations.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination: (a) Client shall pay for all Services rendered through termination date; (b) Provider shall deliver all work product; (c) each Party shall return/destroy the other's Confidential Information.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Fees. Fees for Services are set forth in each SoW or the attached Pricing Schedule.

3.2 Invoicing. Provider shall invoice monthly or per milestone per SoW. Invoices are due Net 30 days from invoice date.

3.3 Late Payment. Overdue amounts bear interest at 1.5% per month (or maximum permitted by law).

3.4 Taxes. Fees exclude applicable taxes (SST 8%). Client is responsible for all applicable taxes.

3.5 Expenses. Pre-approved travel/out-of-pocket expenses reimbursed at cost with receipts.

4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Client IP. Client retains all rights to its pre-existing IP and data provided to Provider.

4.2 Work Product. Upon full payment, all work product (deliverables, code, designs, documentation) created by Provider under this Agreement shall be owned by Client ("Work Product"). Provider assigns all rights to Work Product to Client.

4.3 Provider IP. Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, methodologies, and know-how ("Provider IP"). Client receives a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use Provider IP embedded in Work Product.

4.3 Third-Party IP. Provider shall not incorporate third-party IP without Client's prior written consent and appropriate licenses.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party.

5.2 Obligations. Each Party shall: (a) hold Confidential Information in confidence; (b) not disclose to third parties; (c) use only for performing this Agreement; (d) limit access to need-to-know personnel.

5.3 Exceptions. Not confidential if: publicly available, independently developed, rightfully received from third party, or required by law.

5.3 Term. Confidentiality obligations survive for 2 years post-termination.

6. Data Protection (PDPA)

6.1 Compliance. Both Parties shall comply with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA") and applicable data protection laws.

6.2 Roles. Client is Data Controller; Provider is Data Processor for Client personal data.

6.3 Processing. Provider shall: (a) process personal data only on Client's documented instructions; (b) implement appropriate security measures; (c) not engage sub-processors without Client's written consent; (d) assist Client with data subject requests; (e) notify Client of data breaches within 72 hours.

6.4 DPA. A separate Data Processing Addendum may be executed for detailed obligations.

7. Warranties and Disclaimers

7.1 Provider Warranties. Provider warrants: (a) Services performed professionally; (b) Work Product does not infringe third-party IP; (c) compliance with applicable laws.

7.2 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Cap. Neither Party's aggregate liability shall exceed 12 months' fees paid/payable by Client.

8.2 Exclusions. Neither Party liable for indirect, consequential, punitive, or special damages; loss of profits, data, or business opportunity.

8.3 Exceptions. Cap does not apply to: (a) IP infringement; (b) confidentiality breach; (c) gross negligence/willful misconduct; (d) payment obligations.

8. Indemnification

8.1 By Provider. Provider shall indemnify Client against third-party claims arising from: (a) IP infringement by Work Product; (b) Provider's negligence/willful misconduct; (c) breach of Section 4 or 5.

8.2 By Client. Client shall indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from: (a) Client's content/data; (b) Client's breach of this Agreement.

9. Insurance

Provider shall maintain: (a) Professional Indemnity (min. RM 1,000,000); (b) Commercial General Liability (min. RM 1,000,000); (c) Workers' Compensation as required by law. Certificates provided upon request.

10. Force Majeure

Neither Party liable for failure/delay due to events beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, war, terrorism, strikes, government actions, pandemics). Affected Party shall notify promptly and use reasonable efforts to mitigate.
