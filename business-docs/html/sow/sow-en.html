4. Assumptions & Dependencies

  • Client provides timely access to systems, stakeholders, and documentation
  • Client designates a single point of contact for decisions/approvals
  • Client provides timely feedback (within 3 business days) on deliverables
  • Third-party APIs/services available and documented
  • No changes to scope without formal Change Order

5. Acceptance Criteria

Deliverables are accepted when: (a) all acceptance criteria met; (b) Client signs acceptance form; or (c) 5 business days pass without written objection after delivery.

Client shall test each deliverable within 5 business days. Defects reported in writing. Provider fixes critical defects within 3 business days, major within 5 business days, minor within 10 business days.
