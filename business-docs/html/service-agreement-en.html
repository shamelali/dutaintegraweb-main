2. Scope of Services

The Provider shall deliver the Services as described in the attached Scope Appendix (Annex A), corresponding to the selected tier:

  • Foundation Tier (from RM 2,500/month — helpdesk-only, 6-month startup rate, then RM 3,500/month): 24/7 managed IT support & helpdesk, cloud infrastructure & backups, security monitoring & incident response, PDPA compliance support, monthly system health reports. Note: Dedicated engineer time is not included at Foundation level — available via upgrade to Growth tier or on-request hourly basis.
  • Growth Tier (from RM 5,300/month): All Foundation services, plus sales & workflow automation, content creation & management, ads management, monthly performance reporting, dedicated account manager.
  • AI Partner Tier (custom pricing): All Growth services, plus dedicated AI development budget, priority roadmap, quarterly strategy reviews.
  • Add-ons (per Scope Appendix): Website Development, Website Care, Custom AI Systems, Sales Automation, Content Creation, Ads Management.

The selected tier and any add-ons are specified in the Pricing Schedule (Annex B).

3. Fees & Payment

3.1 Monthly Retainer (Advance Billing)

The Client shall pay the monthly retainer fee as specified in Annex B, invoiced in advance on the 1st of each calendar month, due within 7 days of invoice date. This advance-billing structure ensures timely service delivery and reduces arrears risk for the retainer engagement (confirmed recommendation per audit: C2 billing model — advance + Net 7).

3.2 Onboarding Fee

A one-time onboarding fee of RM {{ONBOARDING_FEE}} is due upon signing. Waived if this Agreement is signed by {{DEADLINE_DATE}} (14 days from proposal date).

3.3 Payment Methods

Bank transfer to Duta Integra Solutions Sdn Bhd (account details on invoice). Late payments incur 1.5% monthly interest.

3.4 Scope Changes

Additional services beyond the Scope Appendix require a written Change Order with agreed pricing before work begins.

4. Term & Termination

4.1 Term

This Agreement commences on the signing date and continues month-to-month.

4.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. The Client pays for all services rendered up to the termination date.

4.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate immediately upon material breach (uncured after 14 days' written notice), insolvency, or force majeure exceeding 60 days.

5. Service Levels & Responsibilities

  • Response commitment (qualitative): Provider will prioritize critical incidents and respond with best efforts during business hours; after-hours response will be handled per the on-call runbook. Specific response-time targets are defined in the Scope Appendix (Annex A) and must be supported by real-time monitoring evidence before being incorporated as binding obligations.
  • Uptime commitment (qualitative): Provider will maintain high availability for managed infrastructure through appropriate technical measures; target uptime levels (e.g., 99.9%) are aspirational benchmarks documented in the security FAQ and must be backed by continuous monitoring logs before being adopted as contract terms.
  • Client provides timely access to systems, credentials, and decision-makers.
  • Client designates a primary point of contact for approvals and communication.

Note (C3 fix — deal blocker resolved per audit recommendation): Numeric SLAs removed from MSA. All performance targets must be evidenced by real monitoring data before inclusion in Annex A (Scope Appendix) or any amendment. The previous §5 numeric commitments (2h/4h response, 99.9% uptime) are not contractually binding.

6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Pre-existing IP: Each party retains ownership of their pre-existing intellectual property.

6.2 Deliverables: Upon full payment, Client owns all custom deliverables created specifically for them under this Agreement (code, content, designs, configurations). Provider retains a non-exclusive license to reuse generic frameworks, libraries, and methodologies.

6.3 Sub-processors & Third-party services: Provider may engage sub-processors (including but not limited to: Vercel, Supabase, Resend, Slack, Cloudflare, AWS, Google Cloud) to deliver the Services, provided that: (a) a current pre-approved sub-processor list is maintained in Annex C (Data Processing Addendum); (b) Client receives 30 days' written notice of any new sub-processor before personal data is shared with it; (c) Client may object to new sub-processors within the 30-day notice period, in which case the Provider will either not use the sub-processor for Client data or negotiate an alternative; (d) Client is responsible for licenses of third-party platforms that are under Client's direct control (e.g., AWS account owned by Client, Meta Ads account). This mechanism ensures full PDPA 2010 (as amended 2024) compliance.

7. Confidentiality

Both parties shall keep confidential all non-public information exchanged (technical, commercial, financial, personal data). Confidentiality survives termination for 3 years. This clause is supplemented by the separate Non-Disclosure Agreement.

8. Data Protection & PDPA

  • Provider acts as Data Processor; Client is Data Controller.
  • Provider implements appropriate technical/organizational measures per Malaysia PDPA 2010 (as amended 2024).
  • Breach notification within 72 hours of discovery.
  • Data Processing Addendum (DPA) attached as Annex C if personal data is processed.

9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages (lost profits, data loss, business interruption).

9.2 Provider's aggregate liability is capped at 12 months' retainer fees paid under this Agreement.

9.3 Liability cap does not apply to: (a) confidentiality breaches, (b) IP infringement, (c) wilful misconduct, (d) data protection fines attributable to Provider's negligence.
