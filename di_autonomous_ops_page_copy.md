## 4. How it works

H2: It watches. It acts — or drafts. You approve.

## 8. FAQ

Q: Is my business data safe?
A: Everything runs inside your existing DI agreement, under PDPA 2010. The agent acts only through permissions you approve, your data stays in your systems, and every single action is logged and visible to you.

Q: What if it gets something wrong?
A: Anything risky requires approval before it happens — so mistakes get caught before they cost you. And when the AI is unsure, it asks. It never guesses on anything that matters.

Q: Do I have to approve everything myself?
A: No. You set the rules. Routine items within your approved allowlist execute automatically; anything outside it goes to the approvals inbox — or to your account manager, if you'd rather not deal with it.

Q: Does this replace my account manager?
A: No — it makes them more useful. The agent handles the repetitive. Your account manager handles judgment, relationships, and the work that genuinely needs a human.

Q: I'm not on a DI plan. Can I still get Autonomous Ops?
A: It runs on DI managed IT, so the natural first step is a free IT audit. We'll show you what the agent would watch, then build from there.
