Our story

Born in Cyberjaya. Built for operators.

Duta Integra Solutions was founded in Cyberjaya in 2023 with a simple mission: make AI and enterprise-grade IT accessible to every Malaysian business — not just the large corporations in KL.

We noticed SMEs across Malaysia were falling behind because they could not find a reliable local technology partner who understood both the business context and the technical depth needed to make real change.

So we built that partner. One that speaks your language, understands PDPA and local compliance, moves fast, and stands behind its work long after the project is delivered.

Today we serve clients across multiple industries and are proud to be 100% Malaysian-owned and operated.

How we operate

A small team, built to cover you properly

We are a boutique studio, not a body shop. That is deliberate — but we know it raises fair questions about coverage. Here is exactly how it works in practice.

Named, dedicated engineer

You work with one engineer who knows your environment — not a different stranger on every ticket.

24/7 monitoring & on-call

Automated monitoring and alerts run around the clock, with an after-hours on-call runbook for incidents.

Weekly check-ins

During builds you get a weekly update: what shipped, what is next, and anything blocked or needing your decision.

Documented, not tribal

Your setup is written down — runbooks, credentials in your name, handover docs — so knowledge never lives in one person's head.
