# Case-study & security metrics — PLEASE CONFIRM BEFORE GO-LIVE

Every number below is a **suggested, realistic Malaysian-SME benchmark** for a
boutique MSP/AI studio of Duta Integra's size. They are written into the case
pages and the Foundation/security FAQ as *targets / typical outcomes*, not as
audited client results.

> Replace any value you cannot stand behind with your real figure, or remove the
> metric line entirely. The site is stronger with three honest numbers than with
> six aspirational ones.

## AGMX — cloud migration & Foundation retainer
- Migration cutover window: **outside business hours, zero weekday downtime**
- Backup RPO: **every 4 hours**
- Backup RTO: **< 4 hours**
- Restore tests: **monthly**
- Uptime target: **99.5%** on managed infrastructure
- Monitoring: **24/7, alerts in < 15 min**

## Eastelpro — WiFi marketing portal + managed IT
- Lead capture at launch: **~60–70% of visitors opt in** (consent-gated)
- Follow-up time: from **manual/none → automated within minutes**
- Network uptime target: **99.5%**
- Support response: **< 1 business hour** for priority issues

## Dutaconnect — RAG AI assistant
- Deflection target: **~60–70%** of repeat enquiries handled automatically
- Accuracy approach: answers grounded in **client-owned docs only**
- Human handoff: for **sensitive / ambiguous / high-value** queries
- Knowledge base: **client-maintained**, updates reflected after re-index

## Leish — custom booking web app
- Mobile share of traffic: **~80%+** (mobile-first build)
- Booking flow: **< 90 seconds** to complete
- Architecture: ready to extend to **payments & automated reminders**

## Foundation / security FAQ (applies across retainers)
- RPO: **from 4 hours**, defined per environment during onboarding
- RTO: **< 4 hours** standard; tighter for critical systems (scoped)
- Restore tests: **scheduled and documented**
- Endpoint protection: **EDR on managed endpoints**
- Monitoring: **24/7 with on-call runbook**
- Incident response: **documented plan, post-incident review**
