About Us

Technology With a Malaysian Heart

We started Duta Integra Solutions because Malaysian SMEs deserve world-class technology without the big-city price tag or the runaround.

Our Story

Duta Integra Solutions was born in Cyberjaya in 2026 with a simple mission: make AI and enterprise-grade IT accessible to every Malaysian business — not just the large corporations in KL.

We noticed that SMEs across Malaysia were falling behind because they couldn't find reliable local technology partners who understood both the business context and the technical depth needed to make real change.

So we built that partner. One that speaks your language, understands PDPA and local compliance, moves fast, and stands behind its work long after the project is delivered.

Today, we serve clients across multiple industries and are proud to be 100% Malaysian-owned and operated.
