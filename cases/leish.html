Leish: a booking platform built around the service, not a theme

A beauty services brand needed booking that matched how customers actually discover and schedule appointments. We built a custom mobile-first web app instead of forcing the business into an off-the-shelf template.

EngagementCustom build
Delivered2024
TypeBooking marketplace
FocusMobile-first

The challenge

Generic booking plugins forced the brand into someone else's flow — awkward on mobile, hard to brand, and impossible to extend. The business wanted discovery and scheduling to feel like its own product, with room to add payments and notifications later.

What we delivered

  • Custom booking flow tailored to the service model and customer journey
  • Mobile-first storefront, since most bookings happen on a phone
  • Clean foundation designed to extend into payments and automated reminders
  • Handover documentation so the brand can keep building on it

The result

Leish launched with a booking experience that matches the brand instead of fighting it. The architecture is set up to grow — payments, notifications, and provider tools can be added without rebuilding from scratch.
