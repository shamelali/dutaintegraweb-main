Eastelpro turns visitor WiFi into a lead channel

A customer-facing business had foot traffic but no way to capture it. We deployed a branded WiFi captive portal and tied it into their sales follow-up, with managed network support underneath.

EngagementWiFi portal + MIT
Delivered2024
StackCaptive portal · CRM
StatusSupported

The challenge

Eastelpro had customers connecting to on-site WiFi every day, but those visitors vanished after they left. There was no branded login, no contact capture, and no follow-up — every visitor was a missed lead.

What we delivered

  • Branded captive portal so visitors log in through the business, not a generic router page
  • Consent-based contact capture aligned with PDPA expectations
  • Lead data routed into the CRM for automated follow-up instead of a spreadsheet
  • Network setup, monitoring, and remote helpdesk folded into ongoing IT support

The result

Foot traffic that used to walk out the door now becomes trackable leads in a pipeline. The network is monitored and supported, so the team is not troubleshooting routers when they should be serving customers.
