The challenge

AGMX, a manufacturing and distribution company, was running on ageing on-premise infrastructure with no formal backup strategy, no monitoring, and a single point of failure. Their previous IT support was reactive — fixing things only when they broke. As the business grew, the risk of extended downtime became unacceptable.

What we did

  • Cloud readiness assessment — audited existing workloads, dependencies, and compliance requirements.
  • Migration planning — designed a phased migration to AWS with zero-weekend-outage execution.
  • Infrastructure as code — provisioned VPC, subnets, security groups, and managed services via Terraform for repeatability.
  • Automated backups — configured daily snapshots with cross-region replication; RPO 4 hours, RTO <4 hours.
  • Monthly restore tests — scheduled and documented restore drills to prove recoverability.
  • 24/7 monitoring & alerting — CloudWatch + custom runbooks; alerts acknowledged within 15 minutes.
  • Incident response runbook — documented escalation paths, communication templates, and post-incident reviews.
  • PDPA-aware controls — data classification, access logging, and retention policies aligned with Malaysia PDPA.

Outcomes

  • Cloud migration planned and executed without a weekend outage
  • Automated backups and restore runbooks in place
  • Retained under Foundation for ongoing monitoring and helpdesk
  • Security monitoring with EDR on managed endpoints
  • Monthly IT health reports delivered to leadership
