Cloud migration · Managed IT

AGMX moves off fragile on-premise infrastructure

A Malaysian SME needed reliable infrastructure without hiring an in-house IT team. We planned and executed a cloud migration, then stayed on to monitor and support it month to month.

EngagementMigration + Foundation
Delivered2024
StackCloud · Backups · EDR
StatusOngoing retainer

The challenge

AGMX was running core business systems on aging on-premise hardware with inconsistent backups and no formal incident response. A single failure could have meant days of downtime, and there was nobody on the team whose job it was to prevent it.

What we delivered

  • Cloud readiness assessment and a documented migration plan with rollback steps
  • Migration executed outside business hours to avoid disruption
  • Automated, tested backups with a defined restore runbook
  • Security monitoring and endpoint protection across the environment
  • Monthly health reports and a dedicated engineer under the Foundation retainer

The result

AGMX now runs on monitored, backed-up infrastructure with a clear point of contact for IT issues. Instead of reacting to failures, the team has predictable monthly support and a path to scale the environment as the business grows.
