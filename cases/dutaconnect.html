Dutaconnect answers the repeat questions so the team does not have to

We built an AI assistant that answers from Dutaconnect's own documentation, embedded it into the existing website, and made sure complex questions still reach a person.

EngagementCustom AI system
Delivered2024
TypeRAG assistant
ChannelWeb + messaging

The challenge

The same questions came in again and again — service details, how things work, basic troubleshooting. Each one pulled the team away from higher-value work, and answers were inconsistent depending on who replied.

What we delivered

  • A retrieval-based assistant grounded in Dutaconnect's own documents, so answers stay factual and on-brand
  • Embedding into the existing website and messaging channels with no rip-and-replace
  • A clear handoff to a human when the question is sensitive, ambiguous, or high-value
  • Documentation and training so the team can update the knowledge base themselves

The result

Common enquiries are handled instantly and consistently, while the team focuses on the conversations that actually need judgement. Because the assistant cites the business's own material, it stays accurate as documentation is updated.
