FAQ

Common questions

Which channels and systems does it connect to?

WhatsApp Business is the primary channel: inbound messages arrive through a signed webhook and replies go out through the platform. Anything that can send an HTTP webhook — n8n, ERPNext, Odoo — can push events in today. Email and CRM sync (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive) are on the roadmap.

Does the AI message prospects on its own?

First contact is human-approved. After that the follow-up agent sends at +4h and +52h within your rules, stops after three attempts, and escalates high-value silence to a person.

How does lead scoring work?

AI extracts five signals — intent, budget, urgency, fit and engagement. Deterministic rules combine them (30/20/20/20/10) into a score of 0–100 and a HOT/WARM/COOL/COLD band. The model never decides the score, and every breakdown is visible on the lead.

Can we customize the follow-up rules?

Yes. Timing, attempt limits, escalation thresholds, language (Bahasa Melayu or English) and approval limits are configurable per tenant.

Is prospect data secure?

Yes. Each tenant's data is isolated and verified with a live RLS probe. When personal data is detected the router forces local inference, so it does not cross borders. Every action lands in the audit log.
