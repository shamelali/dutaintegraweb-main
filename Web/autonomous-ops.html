Process

How it works

1

Observe

AI agents collect signals across your entire infrastructure — servers, apps, cloud, users — in real time.

2

Analyze

Pattern recognition correlates signals to detect anomalies, predict failures, and rank severity.

3

Act

4

You see a clean summary: what happened, what was done, what needs your decision. No noise.

FAQ

Common questions

What does the AI actually control?

Only what you allow. You define the scope — specific servers, apps, or cloud resources. The AI operates within those boundaries and requests approval for anything outside its pre-approved actions.

Can I override or stop the AI?

Yes. Every AI action can be overridden from the dashboard. You can disable any agent instantly, adjust permissions, or switch to fully manual mode at any time.

How is this different from regular monitoring?

Regular monitoring sends alerts. Autonomous Ops takes action — restarts services, scales resources, patches vulnerabilities, blocks threats — then reports what it did. It's monitoring that resolves, not just alerts.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

Every action is logged and reversible. If an AI action causes an issue, our team reviews it within the same business day. Critical actions always require your approval first, so the highest-risk decisions are always yours.

Is my data safe?

Yes. AI agents operate within your environment only. No data leaves your infrastructure. All actions are logged locally and encrypted. We are PDPA-compliant and can sign DPAs for enterprise clients.
