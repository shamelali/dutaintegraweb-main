Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data — in compliance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Last updated: 1 August 2026

1. Who We Are

Duta Integra Solutions Sdn Bhd ("Duta Integra", "we", "us") is a company registered in Malaysia (Company No. [INSERT]). Our registered address is Tamarind Suites, Persiaran Bestari, 63000 Cyberjaya, Selangor. We are the data controller for personal data collected through our website and services.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, job title
  • Enquiry data: Information you provide in contact forms, quizzes, or consultations
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited (collected via analytics)
  • Client portal data: Login credentials (hashed), support tickets, account preferences
  • Infrastructure monitoring data: Anonymized, aggregated system metrics when Autonomous Ops is enabled

3. How We Collect Your Data

  • When you submit a contact form, quiz, or enquiry
  • When you register for a client portal account
  • When you subscribe to Autonomous Ops and provide PDPA consent
  • Automatically through cookies and analytics tools when you browse our website

4. Why We Process Your Data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide requested services
  • To deliver managed IT services, including Autonomous Ops monitoring
  • To send service updates, invoices, and administrative communications
  • To improve our website and services through aggregated analytics
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We rely on your consent (PDPA Section 13) and contractual necessity as the legal bases for processing.

5. Autonomous Ops — Data Handling

When you activate the Autonomous Ops live feed:

  • Only anonymized, aggregated infrastructure data is displayed publicly
  • No client names, IP addresses, hostnames, or sensitive details are ever shown
  • Data is collected at the infrastructure level and stripped of identifiers before storage
  • You may revoke consent and deactivate the feed at any time by contacting us

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: Email delivery (Brevo), hosting (Vercel), analytics (Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics) — under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal authorities: When required by Malaysian law or court order

7. Data Retention

  • Enquiry data: Retained for 2 years from last contact
  • Client portal data: Retained for the duration of the service contract plus 1 year
  • Autonomous Ops feed data: Retained for 90 days, then automatically purged
  • Analytics data: Retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default)

8. Your Rights Under PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Section 30)
  • Correct inaccurate data (Section 34)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Section 33)
  • Request deletion of your data (Section 34)
  • Data portability (Section 34A)

To exercise these rights, email us at info@dutaintegra.my. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, regular security assessments, and staff training. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we follow industry best practices.

10. International Transfers

Some of our service providers (e.g., Vercel, Google) may store data outside Malaysia. Where data is transferred internationally, we ensure adequate protection through contractual clauses or the provider's compliance with recognised data protection frameworks.

11. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and optional analytics cookies (Vercel Analytics, and Google Analytics when enabled). You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to active clients and published on this page with an updated effective date.
