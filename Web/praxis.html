FAQ

Common questions

What processes can Praxis automate?

Any repeatable workflow: approvals, onboarding, invoicing, reporting, and more. If it follows rules and steps, Praxis can automate it — from simple single-step tasks to complex cross-department orchestration.

Does it replace our existing tools?

No, it integrates with your current systems. Praxis sits on top of your existing tools — ERPs, CRMs, email, spreadsheets — and orchestrates between them without requiring you to rip and replace.

How long does setup take?

1–2 weeks for most businesses. We map your existing processes first, then configure automation rules. More complex workflows may take longer, but you'll see value from day one.

Can we customize approval chains?

Yes, fully configurable multi-level approvals. Set conditions for who approves what, when escalations trigger, and how parallel approvals work — all without writing code.

Is our data secure?

Yes. PDPA-compliant, data stays in your environment. AI processes data in-context without exporting it. All actions are logged, encrypted, and auditable.
