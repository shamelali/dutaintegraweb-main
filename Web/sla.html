Service Level Agreement

Our commitment to reliability, response times, and accountability for every managed IT service we deliver.

Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Uptime Guarantee

99.9%
Uptime SLA

We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all managed infrastructure during each calendar month, measured as the percentage of minutes in which monitored systems are available and responsive. Scheduled maintenance windows are excluded from uptime calculations and communicated at least 48 hours in advance.

If uptime falls below 99.9% in any month, clients are eligible for service credits as described in Section 6.

Response time is measured from the moment an incident is reported or an automated alert is triggered. First update is the time by which you will receive a substantive status update with initial assessment. Target resolution is our best-effort timeframe for resolving the issue or providing a viable workaround.

4. Escalation Path

If an issue is not resolved within the target timeframe, the following escalation applies:

  • Level 1: Assigned engineer — standard support
  • Level 2: Senior engineer + team lead — within 2× target resolution time
  • Level 3: Technical director + client notification — within 3× target resolution time
  • Level 4: Executive escalation — immediate involvement of Duta Integra management

For P1 incidents, escalation begins at Level 2 immediately upon classification.

5. Monitoring & Reporting

  • 24/7 automated monitoring of all managed systems
  • Real-time alerts to on-call engineers for P1 and P2 events
  • Weekly health reports for all clients
  • Monthly SLA compliance reports available on request
  • Quarterly business reviews for Growth and AI Partner clients

6. Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, service credits are applied as follows:

Uptime RangeCredit (% of monthly fee)
99.0% – 99.9%5%
95.0% – 99.0%10%
90.0% – 95.0%25%
Below 90.0%50% (maximum credit)

Credits are applied to the next invoice. To claim a credit, clients must notify us within 30 days of the month in which the shortfall occurred.

7. Exclusions

This SLA does not apply to downtime caused by:

  • Scheduled maintenance (communicated 48 hours in advance)
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, government actions, internet outages)
  • Client-side issues (local network, ISP, client hardware)
  • Third-party services outside our control (SaaS providers, DNS registrars)
  • Actions taken at the client's explicit request

8. Autonomous Ops Add-on SLA

Clients subscribed to the Autonomous Ops add-on receive additional SLA commitments:

  • AI response time: Automated actions within 5 minutes of detection
  • Human escalation: Within 15 minutes for P1 events not auto-resolved
  • Feed uptime: 99.95% availability of the real-time aggregate feed
  • Data retention: 90 days of event history accessible via dashboard

9. Changes to This SLA

We may update this SLA with 30 days' written notice to active clients. Material changes to response times or credit terms require client consent.
