FAQ

Common questions

What training can Sensai manage?

Any corporate training: compliance, technical skills, onboarding, soft skills. Sensai adapts to your existing content library and can curate external resources that align with your learning objectives.

Does it create training content?

It curates and recommends; you provide the core content. Sensai organizes your existing materials, identifies gaps, and suggests supplementary resources — but your subject matter experts retain control over what's taught.

Can employees see their own progress?

Yes, full transparency for learners. Every employee has access to their own dashboard showing completed modules, upcoming deadlines, skill progression, and recommended next steps.

How does it personalize learning?

AI adapts pace, content type, and difficulty based on performance. If an employee struggles with a concept, Sensai provides additional resources and adjusts the learning path accordingly.

Is employee data secure?

Yes, PDPA-compliant, individual data is private and encrypted. Employee learning records are stored securely and only accessible to the employee and authorized administrators.
