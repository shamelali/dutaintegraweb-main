# DutaConnect

## Overview

DutaConnect is Duta Integra's AI-powered client flow and sales automation platform. It handles lead scoring, follow-up sequences, meeting scheduling, and deal pipeline management — with human oversight on every sales decision.

## Key Features

- **Lead Scoring** — AI ranks prospects by conversion probability
- **Follow-up Sequences** — Automated, personalized outreach campaigns
- **Meeting Scheduling** — AI books meetings based on availability
- **Deal Pipeline** — Real-time deal tracking and forecasting
- **Email Integration** — Sync with Gmail, Outlook, CRM systems
- **Sales Analytics** — Conversion rates, pipeline health, rep performance

## How It Works

1. **Connect** — Link your CRM, email, and calendar
2. **Score** — AI analyzes and ranks all leads automatically
3. **Engage** — Automated follow-ups and meeting booking
4. **Close** — You focus on closing, AI handles the process
