# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is Duta Integra?

Duta Integra is a Malaysian AI and IT solutions company helping SMEs modernize, automate, and grow through smart technology. We offer managed IT services, AI development, and specialized automation products.

### What products do you offer?

- **Flowmail** — AI email automation
- **Praxis** — Business process automation
- **Ontrac** — Operational workflow management
- **Sensai** — Intelligent learning systems
- **Integrall** — Autonomous AI agent operations
- **DutaConnect** — Client flow & sales automation

### How do I get started?

Visit our [contact page](https://dutaintegra.my/contact.html) or email info@dutaintegra.my. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your needs and recommend the right solution.

## Products

### Can I use multiple products together?

Yes! All products are designed to work independently or as an integrated suite. Many clients use 2-3 products together for maximum automation.

### Are your products suitable for small businesses?

Absolutely. Our products are designed for SMEs (5-200 employees). We offer flexible pricing and can start with a single product and expand as you grow.

### Do you offer free trials?

We offer a pilot program where you can test any product on a single use case before committing. Contact us to arrange a pilot.

## Technical

### What systems do you integrate with?

- **Email** — Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, any IMAP
- **CRM** — Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive
- **ERP** — SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics
- **Cloud** — AWS, Azure, GCP
- **Custom** — Any system with API access

### Is my data secure?

Yes. We are PDPA-compliant with:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Data residency in Malaysia
- Role-based access control
- Full audit trails

### Can I customize the AI behavior?

Yes. All products allow you to:
- Set approval rules and thresholds
- Define scope and permissions
- Configure escalation paths
- Adjust AI confidence levels
