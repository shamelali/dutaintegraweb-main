# Audit-to-Retainer Conversion Playbook

14-day workflow for turning free brand-audit leads into Foundation / Growth clients.
Built around: Foundation RM 2,000 · Growth RM 5,300 · AI Partner · add-ons (Website Care, Custom AI).

---

## Day 0 — Lead lands

Audit completed on /audit, contact captured via the register modal.

- WhatsApp within **1 hour**: "Your site scored X/100. I found 3 things worth 15 minutes of your time — when are you free today or tomorrow?"
- **Never send the full report before the call.** The score is the hook; the walkthrough is the value.

## Day 1–2 — The 20-minute debrief call

| Min | Action |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Their score + grade on screen. Plain-terms meaning. |
| 3–15 | **Top 3 issues only.** Each translated to risk or cost (formulas below). |
| 15–18 | Map each issue to the exact retainer line that fixes it (table below). |
| 18–20 | Close: "I'll send a proposal today addressing these 3 findings. Agreement + NDA attached — one signature and we start. Signed within 14 days = onboarding fee waived." |

### Risk / cost translation formulas

- **Security / HTTPS / stale plugins** → "Under the amended PDPA, a reportable breach is up to a RM 1,000,000 fine."
- **Slow load** → "Every extra second, ~10% of visitors leave. RM 1,000/mo ad spend = RM 300+ evaporating before they see your offer."
- **SEO gaps** → "You're paying Google for customers who should find you free."
- **Manual follow-up** → "You're spending ~X hours/month retyping the same WhatsApp replies — RM Y in wages doing a robot's job."
- **No backups / downtime** → "One ransomware incident = RM 5–15k recovery, or the business just stops."

### Finding → Tier mapping

| Audit finding | Maps to |
|---|---|
| Security, HTTPS, PDPA gaps | **Foundation** — security monitoring, PDPA compliance support |
| Downtime / backup risk | **Foundation** — cloud infra & backups, 24/7 helpdesk |
| Slow / broken site | **Website Care add-on** → Foundation health reports |
| Manual sales follow-up | **Growth** — sales & workflow automation (RM 1,500 line) |
| Stale content / blog | **Growth** — content creation (RM 1,000 line) |
| Ads waste / no visibility | **Growth** — ads management (RM 800 line) |
| "A chatbot would help" | **AI Partner** or Custom AI Systems add-on |

## Day 2 — Proposal (within 24h of call)

Use `docs/proposal-template.html` (open in browser, fill fields, print to PDF). 3 pages:

1. **Cover + scorecard** — their score, grade, domain, date.
2. **The money table** — Finding → What it costs you → Deliverable that fixes it → Fixed by week X.
3. **Scope + close** — tier scope, first-30-days plan, pricing with deadline date printed, signature block. Service Agreement + NDA attached so signing is one step.

## Follow-up cadence

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 2 | Proposal lands. WhatsApp: "Sent — page 2 is the one to look at." |
| 4 | Value nudge, not a chase: "We already fixed [small thing] on sites like yours." |
| 9 | Deadline reminder: "Onboarding-fee waiver ends [date]." |
| 14 | Final: "Closing your audit file tomorrow. If now's not the time, Website Care is the low-risk entry — free re-audit in 6 months." |
| 15+ | Newsletter nurture. Dead, not deleted. |

## Rules

1. **Max 3 findings on the call.** 10 overwhelms; 3 creates a solvable problem.
2. **Quote their numbers, not yours** — their score, their URL, their hours. The retainer reads as the answer key to a test they just watched themselves fail.

## SLA / tracking

- Proposal out within 24h of call — no exceptions.
- Log every lead: source, score, call date, proposal date, close date, tier.
- Review close rate monthly; if <20%, the debrief call script is the first thing to fix.
