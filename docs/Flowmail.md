# Flowmail

## Overview

Flowmail is Duta Integra's AI-powered email automation platform. It handles smart inbox triage, draft replies, follow-up scheduling, and campaign optimization — all with human oversight on every send decision.

## Key Features

- **Smart Inbox Triage** — Auto-categorize, priority sort, VIP detection
- **AI Draft Replies** — Context-aware responses in your tone
- **Follow-up Automation** — Never miss a follow-up again
- **Campaign Optimization** — A/B testing, send-time optimization
- **Template Intelligence** — Auto-personalize templates per recipient
- **Analytics Dashboard** — Open rates, response times, sentiment

## How It Works

1. **Connect** — Link your email (Gmail, Outlook, any IMAP)
2. **Learn** — AI learns your tone, priorities, and patterns
3. **Automate** — AI handles routine emails, drafts responses
4. **Approve** — Review and approve before sending
