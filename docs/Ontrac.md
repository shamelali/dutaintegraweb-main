# Ontrac

## Overview

Ontrac is Duta Integra's AI-powered operational workflow management platform. It provides real-time tracking, resource allocation, performance monitoring, and bottleneck detection — with human oversight on operational changes.

## Key Features

- **Real-time Tracking** — Live visibility into all operational processes
- **Resource Allocation** — AI optimizes workforce and resource distribution
- **Performance Monitoring** — KPI dashboards with anomaly detection
- **Bottleneck Detection** — Identify and resolve process constraints
- **Automated Reporting** — Generate operational reports automatically
- **SLA Management** — Track and enforce service level agreements

## How It Works

1. **Integrate** — Connect your operational systems and data sources
2. **Monitor** — AI tracks all operations in real-time
3. **Alert** — Intelligent alerts for deviations and bottlenecks
4. **Report** — Automated summaries and optimization recommendations
