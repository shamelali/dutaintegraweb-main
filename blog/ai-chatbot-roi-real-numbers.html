AI chatbot ROI: real numbers from real builds

Duta Integra Solutions · Cyberjaya · 7 min read

Every AI vendor has a ROI slide. Very few show the working. Here is the honest arithmetic we share with Malaysian SME clients before they commission a chatbot — including where the savings are real and where they evaporate.

The cost side (what you actually pay)

  • Build: a scoped customer-service bot — trained on your FAQ, product list and policies, wired to WhatsApp and your website — typically starts around RM 18k for SMEs. Scope, not hype, drives price: number of intents, integrations and languages.
  • Run: hosting plus LLM API usage. For a business handling a few hundred conversations a month this is usually a low three-figure monthly bill, not a five-figure one.
  • Maintenance: someone must review conversations weekly for the first month or two, correct wrong answers, and update content when prices or policies change. Budget 2–4 hours a week initially.

Where hours genuinely come back

  1. Repetitive pre-sales questions. "Do you deliver to Johor?" "What are your hours?" "How much is X?" These are 50–70% of inbound messages in retail and services. A bot that answers instantly, 24/7, removes most of them from your team's queue.
  2. Lead capture outside office hours. Enquiries at 11pm used to be cold by morning. A bot that qualifies and books them keeps the conversation warm. This is revenue recovery, not just cost saving.
  3. Internal lookups. The underrated win: a bot that answers staff questions ("what's our refund policy?", "which client uses which SLA?") saves minutes dozens of times a day across the whole team.

A worked example (directional, not promised)

Say your two-person admin team spends three hours a day on repetitive enquiries. That is roughly six hours a day of attention. If a chatbot absorbs even half, you free about 45–50 hours a month. At a loaded cost of RM 25/hour that is around RM 1,100–1,250/month of capacity — before counting after-hours leads captured. Against typical SME build-plus-run costs, simple bots reach break-even inside the first year, faster if lead volume is meaningful.

The honest ROI case is not "replace staff". It is: stop paying humans to copy-paste answers, and point that attention at work only humans can do.

How to sanity-check any vendor's claim

  • Ask what percentage of conversations the bot resolves end-to-end — and what happens with the rest. Handover design matters more than model choice.
  • Demand a pilot scope. Two weeks on your top 20 questions, measured against your current response times.
  • Check language fit. Malaysian customers mix English, Bahasa Malaysia and Manglish. Test it with real transcripts, not marketing demos.
  • Insist on transcript access. You should be able to read every conversation and export analytics. No access means no accountability.
